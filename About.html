About Kids' Fun Day

Our History

Kids' Fun Day is a non-profit organisation created to encourage children to participate in outdoor activities, teamwork and healthy competition.

The event is organised for children in Ward 13 Kwa Novunula during the festive season.

Our Mission

To create an interactive digital platform that connects children, parents, guardians, sponsors and volunteers by providing information about Kids' Fun Day activities.

Our Vision

To become the leading community event that inspires healthy, active and united children in Ward 13.

Our Target Audience

  • Children aged 9-11 years
  • Parents and guardians
  • Ward 13 community members
  • Event sponsors
  • Volunteers
