Event Information

Kids' Fun Day provides children with an opportunity to participate in outdoor games and activities while developing teamwork, communication and problem-solving skills.

Team Requirements

  • Each team must have 10 children.
  • 5 males and 5 females.
  • Teams must be from Ward 13.

Team Colours

Each team must choose one colour to represent their area:

  • Yellow
  • Green
  • Blue
  • Red
  • Orange
  • Purple

Games and Scoring

There will be five main games. Each of the five games is worth 10 points.

A final game will be held and it is worth 50 points.

Game Points
Game 1 10
Game 2 10
Game 3 10
Game 4 10
Game 5 10
Final Game 50
